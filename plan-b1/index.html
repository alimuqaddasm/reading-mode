Four faults are common to all fifteen, and they are the same four S01 was cured of:

  1. No everyday moment at the door. Every lesson opens with «پچھلے سبق میں دیکھا...» and a statement of what the lesson will say. S01 opens at the mirror.
  2. No controlling image. The sections are a row of topics; S01 carries the mirror from the first line to the last narration.
  3. The lesson's own voice is thin and mechanical. S01 has 35 linking sentences that raise each question from the last answer. S02 to S16 have 11 to 16, and in every section the last of them is the same formula, «اخلاق میں: ...». That one refrain accounts for about half of each lesson's own words and reads as a stamp, not as a landing.
  4. Scholars are stacked, not woven. Sections run «MM line, AH line, EZ line, JS line» on one point, so the same thing is said three times in three voices; and short sections of one to four lines stand alone (S05 section 1, S06 section 3, S08 sections 2, 4 and 7, S12 section 8).
