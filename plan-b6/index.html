The steps 7
  1. Can the eye see God at all? No: He has no limit, so the eye does not reach Him; He is not seen, heard, sensed or touched, and that is why He is God; whatever is sensed is not God, for He is of the genus neither of the sensed nor of the intellected. (MM P001:5 Fiqh al-Rida; AH P053:1-2, P053:8-18 with «لا يحس ولا يجس» P053:6-8; U6.1/1, U6.1/32). [U6.1/1 core repeat kept in full: the bare claim is in B4 S06, B2 S02, B3 S03 in passing; here it is the Bab's thesis | U6.1/32 PART: recall B3 S10 "al-Rida to the zindiq: because the senses do not perceive Him He is a thing unlike things, our Lord", then new: AH's telling to Imran the Sabian and "not of the genus of the sensed nor of the intellected", in AH P053:1-2, P053:14-18; Rule 7, flagged]
  2. What does the Quran say? ﴿لَا تُدْرِكُهُ الْأَبْصَارُ وَهُوَ يُدْرِكُ الْأَبْصَارَ﴾ (6:103) as the Fasl's one ayah block: He sees everything and is not seen. (AH P009:2; U6.1/1). [part of the U6.1/1 core repeat: B4 S06 printed 6:102-103 for the Essence not determined by sight; printed again here, flagged]
  3. Do His very names say it? «هو» points to the One absent from the reach of sight and the touch of the senses; «الله» is the One veiled from the reach of sight, imaginations and thoughts. (MM P008:1, P008:2; JS P011:3-7 «ضمیر شأن» fine layer; U6.1/2, Teacher Copy layer per the ledger). ↺ B2 S02Already taught: B2 S02 "the breath example: the ha of Huwa alerts to the Fixed (AH, Urdu only)",New here: Huwa read as naming the One absent from all perception, in MM P008:1 and the vision clause of P008:2

No: He is absolutely unseen to every outer and inner faculty, yet proven by reason; minds, thoughts and hearts do not perceive His attribute; the windows of sight fall short before His essence. The steps 6
  1. Can God be seen with the eye at all? No: the eyes did not see Him, the hearts saw Him by the realities of faith; whatever sight can reach is created and needs a creator, and whoever likens Him to His creation has set up a partner with God (MM U6.3-1 P001:3-7, cut at «فقد اتخذ مع الله شريكا»; JS P003:2-13 in one line, «محدود ہوا تو مخلوق ہوا»; AH P002:5-9 the eye encloses it, so it is weaker than the eye, P002:1-3, 12-13; U6.3/1). [PART, kept in full as core repeat: recall B5 S03 "whatever imagination distinguishes is created like you" then the rule applied to sight and likening named shirk]
  2. Why does being seen make a thing created? Seen means bounded, with a place and a direction, so needy; the Self-subsistent needs nothing; and reason alone proves it, apart from the texts (EZ P016:19-23; JS P005:8-9, P020:1-12 as the one statement of the chain, P010:12-27 one line; P023:17-23, P024:3-21; EZ P017:16-21 "every limit is a defect" with 37:180 inline; sermon block P023:2-4 with P023:9-16; pulled JS U6.1-2 P041:1-2 and EZ U6.1-1 P022:33-37; U6.3/2, /3).   3. Is there a simpler proof? If He were visible we would see Him, since the conditions the opponent sets for seeing (sound sight, no barrier) are present; we self-evidently do not, so He is not visible; and 6:103 says it (JS P020:13-15, P042:1-11; U6.3/12). new

  5. Why must seer and seen be alike? Seeing needs air for sight to pass through, and light, between seer and seen; sharing the cause of seeing makes them alike (ishtibah = likeness), and that is tashbih; since He resembles no creature, He is not visible (JS U6.3-2 P052:7-12, block extended to «وكان في ذلك التشبيه»; P044:3-21; the footnote's syllogism P053:2-22 in Urdu, Teacher Copy; pulled JS U6.1-1 P028:7-8; U6.3/18, /14, /19). If He were seen, once the gaze left Him His state of facing and being seen would alter, so He would change; whatever changes is not the absolutely rich; and a supposition that entails the false is itself false (JS U6.3-1 P021:4 block once, P021:5-6, P033:1-12, P037:4-16; U6.3-2 P045:6-22; U6.3/5, /15).
